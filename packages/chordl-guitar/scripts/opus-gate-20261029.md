## Reproduction and independence

The original checkers are copied into `opus-v2/` with I/O adaptations only: full-cohort evaluation, correct rooted recovery bookkeeping, and explicit unavailable rows. Their musical check, hand, search, score and comparison functions are unchanged. `opus-v2/adaptations.json` records source and adapted hashes. The independent verifier runs with `python3 -I`; result: **pass** over 3195 rows. Original inputs and v1 output remain untouched (`*-v1.*`).

This is heuristic verification, not a guitar playing/listening test. Residual regressions are retained in `opus-v2/regressions.md` and `verdicts.json`, including any legacy checker limitation on non-index mini-barres. No chordcards source, live data or uploads are involved.
