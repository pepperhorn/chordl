# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Add `m7b5(b9)` and `dim7(b9)` on all 15 catalogue roots (30 chords).
  Slug tails are `m7-flat5-flat9` and `dim7-flat9`; aliases include
  `m7(b5,b9)`, `m7b5b9`, `ø7(b9)`, `dim7b9` and `°7(b9)`.
- Keep the diminished seventh's teaching spelling as the major sixth,
  including in `displayNoteName` for `dim7b9` voicing ids.
- Release after `chordl-core@0.6.1`: push `chordl-core@0.6.1`, wait for
  successful publication, then push `chordl-cards@0.3.0` on the merged main
  commit. Tag pushes and publication are separate owner actions.
